BMI Calculator

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height² (m).

BSA Calculator

Mosteller: BSA = √[(height × weight) ÷ 3600].

Ideal Body Weight

Devine formula: male 50 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft; female 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft.

Glasgow Coma Scale

APGAR Score

Anion Gap

Anion gap = Na⁺ − (Cl⁻ + HCO₃⁻). This version does not include potassium.

Corrected Calcium

Common estimate: corrected Ca = measured Ca + 0.8 × (4.0 − albumin).

EDD Calculator

Naegele's rule: LMP + 280 days (40 weeks), assuming a reliable LMP and typical cycle length.

Mean Arterial Pressure

Approximate MAP = DBP + ⅓(SBP − DBP).
